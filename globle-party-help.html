🎉 How to Play Globle Party

The world geography party game. Everyone plays on their own phone, with an optional big screen for the whole room to watch. Three games, teams if you want them, and a live leaderboard.

🎯 Quiz ShowA country glows. Everyone answers. Fastest right answer scores most. 🔔 Buzzer BattleFirst to buzz gets to answer. Get it wrong and others can steal. 🌡️ Hot & Cold RaceRace to find the mystery country on your own globe.
📺

Getting started

Set up a game in under a minute.

🏠 Two ways to host

📺

Host on this screen

Open the game on a laptop or TV and it becomes the big shared screen showing the globe, the answers and the leaderboard. The host joins on their phone too, so they can play.

📱

Host from my phone (no TV)

No big screen needed. Every phone shows the globe and the results, and the host runs the game from their phone while playing at the same time.

👋 Joining

  1. Scan the QR code on the big screen, or open the game and tap Join a game and type the 4-letter room code.
  2. Pick a name, an animal avatar and a colour. They're remembered for next time.
  3. Wait in the lobby. You'll see everyone who's joined. If teams are on, you can pick your team here.

Best with 3–6 players, but it works with 12 or more. The screens shrink to fit everyone.

⚙️ Lobby settings (host)

Game: Quiz Show, Buzzer Battle or Hot & Cold Race.

Answers: Multiple choice (four coloured buttons) or Typing (type the name or pick it from the list). One style for the whole game. Not used in the Race.

Rounds: how many countries to play (5–20, or 3–10 for the Race).

Timer: seconds to answer in Quiz Show, seconds to buzz in Buzzer Battle, or a round time limit of 2–5 minutes in the Race.

Teams: off, or 2, 3 or 4 teams. See Teams below.

🎯

Quiz Show

Everyone answers every question. Speed counts.

🎯 How it works

  1. A country glows purple. The globe swings round so you can see the continent, pauses, then zooms in. Tiny countries get a pulsing ring.
  2. Answer on your phone before the timer runs out, by tapping one of four choices or typing the name.
  3. Drumroll… Once everyone's locked in (or time's up), see how everyone answered, then the right answer turns green.
  4. Leaderboard. Watch the rankings shuffle, then it's on to the next country.

The faster you answer, the more you score, so trust your gut!

🔔

Buzzer Battle

Only the fastest buzzer gets a shot.

🔔 How it works

BUZZ!
  1. A country glows and every phone shows a big red BUZZ! button.
  2. Buzz if you know it. The fastest buzz wins the right to answer, and the big screen shows who buzzed first and by how much.
  3. Answer quickly. You get 7 seconds to tap one of four choices, or 12 seconds to type it.
  4. Right? You score. Wrong (or out of time)? You're locked out of that country and it's a STEAL! The buzzers reopen for everyone else.
  5. If nobody gets it before time runs out, the answer is revealed and nobody scores.

⚡ Scoring & rules

Right answer after a lightning-fast buzzup to 1,000
Right answer after a slow buzz500+
Countries won in a row+100 each, up to +500
Wrong answer0, and you're out for that country

No jumping the gun. A buzz button appears during the countdown too. Tap it before the country appears and you're locked out for 2 seconds.

Steals: in multiple choice, the wrong answer is crossed out for whoever steals.

With teams: a wrong answer locks out the whole team, so the steal goes to another team.

🌡️

Hot & Cold Race

Everyone hunts the same mystery country. First to find it wins the round.

🌡️ How it works

  1. Everyone gets their own globe on their phone with the same hidden mystery country. Drag to spin it, pinch or scroll to zoom.
  2. Guess any country. It's coloured by how close it is to the mystery country, and you get a message: 🔥 Warmer or 🧊 Colder.
  3. Follow the heat. Your guesses are listed closest first, with the distance in km.
  4. Find it! It turns green and you're done. The moment the first person finds it, everyone else has 60 seconds left.
  5. The round ends when that countdown runs out, when everyone has found it, or when the round time limit is reached.
🧊 Far awayBordering 🔥

Distance is measured edge to edge, so a country that shares a border with the answer shows as "Bordering", the hottest colour. Watch out for overseas territories: France's islands make it "near" all sorts of places.

📡 The radar

The big screen shows a radar of everyone in the race. Each avatar creeps towards the middle as that player's closest guess gets nearer. Badges show how many guesses they've made. Nobody's actual guesses are shown until the round ends.

No big screen? Tap the 📡 radar button at the top of your phone for a mini version.

You'll also get pop-ups on your phone when someone is bordering the country or finds it, so you know when to hurry!

At the end of the round, the big screen shows everyone's guesses on the globe in hot and cold colours, all around the answer.

🤝

Teams

Works with all three games.

🤝 Playing in teams

Set Teams to 2, 3 or 4 in the lobby: 🦘 Kangaroo, 🥝 Kiwi, 🦜 Toucan and 🐧 Penguin. New players go to the smallest team automatically. Players can switch team on their phone, and the host can tap a player on the big screen to move them, or hit 🔀 Shuffle teams.

Team score = the average of its players' scores, so a team of 2 can beat a team of 4.

Team bonus: in Quiz Show and the Race, if everyone on a team gets it right (or finds it), the team gets +100.

Between rounds, two teams battle in a tug of war, and three or four teams go head to head in a team race. There's a Round MVP too.

👑 Host controls

Skip ▶ moves straight to the next step. Handy if someone wandered off mid-question.

Play again on the final podium takes everyone back to the lobby with the same players, ready to pick a new game.

End game closes the room for everyone.

❓ Quick questions

My phone dropped out. Can I get back in?

Yes. Open the game again in the same browser and tap Rejoin, or scan the QR code again. You keep your score.

Two of us tried to join from the same laptop

Each browser counts as one player, so two tabs in the same browser are the same person. Use separate devices, or a private/incognito window for the second player.

It says "This game has been updated"

Your device had an older saved copy of the game. Tap Update now and you'll be put straight back in the same room.

The big screen froze or went to sleep

The hosting screen (or the host's phone with no TV) runs the game's timers. The game asks it to stay awake, but keep it plugged in and on the game's tab. If it does sleep, wake it up and the game carries on.

It doesn't recognise the name I typed

Most common names work, like "USA", "UK", "Holland", "Burma", "Ivory Coast" and "DRC". If you're unsure, start typing and pick from the suggestions, or tap the empty box to scroll through all 195 countries.

Which countries are in the game?

All 195 recognised countries: the 193 UN members plus Vatican City and Palestine.
